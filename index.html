PT · Business Concept Development

Who MIRA is,
why she exists.

Mission

To make technology more approachable and accessible through innovative, reliable, and user-friendly solutions.

Vision

To be the most recognizable and trusted friendly tech brand where every interaction feels simple, human, and joyful, and technology feels approachable for everyone.

Goals

To establish MIRA as a recognizable and memorable brand through a distinctive logo, consistent visual identity, and friendly brand personality.

Values

MIRA values innovation, simplicity, reliability, creativity, and accessibility. It aims to combine modern technology with a friendly design that creates a positive and engaging user experience.

Brand conceptualizing

MIRA is a friendly and modern robot brand designed to represent intelligence, innovation, and simplicity.

Full concept

Inspired by its cute robot logo, Mira aims to create a recognizable identity that makes technology feel more approachable, engaging, and user-friendly. The blue color palette symbolizes trust and innovation, while the bold black outlines emphasize strength and clarity. Its friendly facial expression adds personality, making Mira appear welcoming and memorable.

Task PT

Brand identity brief

To develop a unique and cohesive brand identity for MIRA that reflects its identity as a modern, intelligent, and approachable robot brand. The design should emphasize simplicity, creativity, and professionalism through a clean logo, a consistent blue, black, and white color palette, and recognizable visual elements. The overall goal is to create a memorable brand that communicates innovation while maintaining a friendly and accessible image.
